# 3.27 compatibility notes

- Exact tested firmware: reMarkable OS 3.27.1.0 on reMarkable 2.
- The artifact has an exact `VERSION 3.27.1.0` guard. No broader 3.27 compatibility is claimed.
- qt-resource-rebuilder v17 was the only active XOVI extension during testing.
- AppLoad and KOReader were no longer installed according to the user, so their regression matrix was not applicable on 3.27.
- LAMY-button, split-screen, and other third-party-mod coexistence were not tested and are not claimed.
- Stock 3.27 emitted a pre-existing `Experimental.qml: Values is not defined` warning with and without this QMD.
- Stock startup also retained a previously known `unhandled thickness 8` warning from persisted tool state. It did not crash. No `unhandled thickness 100` warning appeared in the 3.27 tests.
