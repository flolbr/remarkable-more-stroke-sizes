The first 3.27 candidate exposed the eight non-100 entries but omitted their text because 3.27 delegates require `thicknessModel.translationContext()`. Adding that method restored all labels.

Thickness 100 was then introduced as a separately hashed experiment because it had caused a perceived hang on 3.24. On 3.27.1.0 it passed selection, drawing, navigation, xochitl/XOVI restart, full reboot, removal, and reinstall checks without a hang or `unhandled thickness 100` warning. It was therefore promoted into the final 3.27 source.
