# 3.27 localization test summary

Date: 2026-08-08
Firmware: 3.27.1.0
Hardware: reMarkable 2
QMD SHA-256: `ad731ad0c846cf2fd7443754fa5947579583921b1028996d331d39b8694b3333`

## Policy

- Every label appends its numeric thickness in parentheses.
- Project-owned label tables cover English, French, German, and Spanish.
- Unknown languages fall back to the non-blank English table.
- The language detector uses verified stock translations of `Thin` from
  `xofm::libs::toolbar::PenThicknessModel`: `Thin`, `Fin`, `Schmal`, and
  `Fino`.
- The delegate uses the project-specific `MoreStrokeSizes` context so the
  selected project label is not translated again.

## Physical results

- PASS: English/default labels, including `Can't Go Thicker (100)`.
- PASS: French labels, selection/drawing subset, tool switching, and notebook
  reopen.
- PASS: German labels, including `Dicker geht's nicht (100)`, plus the
  selection/drawing subset.
- PASS: Spanish labels, including `¡No puede ser más grueso! (100)`, plus the
  selection/drawing subset.
- PASS: no blank labels or reported clipping in any tested language.
- PASS: `0.5`, `1`, `2.5`, `8`, and `100` retained their numeric behavior.
- PASS: QMLDiff compatibility and byte-for-byte hash/unhash round trip.

## Diagnostics and rejected approaches

The original shortened context `PenThicknessModel` did not translate any
candidate string. The exact stock context translated only the stock keys:
`Thin`, `Medium`, and `Thick`.

`Qt.locale().name` reported `en_US` while xochitl had activated French, so it
was not used. A diagnostic instance of xochitl's `LanguageSettings` QML type
left the display on a restart screen; XOVI was stopped immediately, stock
xochitl recovered with zero failed units, and that approach was removed.
