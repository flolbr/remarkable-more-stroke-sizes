# Repository instructions

- Keep readable QMD files under `src/` as the source of truth.
- Regenerate files under `patches/` with QMLDiff and the exact firmware hashtable; do not edit hashed identifiers manually.
- Do not commit firmware, hashtables, extracted proprietary QML, device backups, user documents, credentials, or private logs.
- Do not broaden firmware compatibility claims without physical testing on the claimed build.
- Preserve the upstream MIT license and attribution.
